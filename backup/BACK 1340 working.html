Welcome to 1340

1340 is more than a cafe — it's Savar's premier destination for handcrafted mocktails, single-origin coffee, and modern Western cuisine. Born from a passion for bringing world-class flavors to Dhaka's heart, every drink and dish is a story.

Our signature mocktails feature Monin premium syrups — the same flavors trusted by top bartenders globally. From our bold black coffee to our sizzling grilled steaks, every item on our menu is made from scratch with the finest ingredients.

Whether you're here for an evening with friends, a quiet coffee, or a late-night steak craving — 1340 is your place.
